Core Capabilities

Everything Your Phone Needs to Think for Itself

OpenDroid combines autonomous planning, deep device integration, and multi-modal AI into one seamless agent.

Habit & Routine Detection

Mines repeated daily app workflows and proactively offers one-click automated routines with morning briefings.

Telegram & WhatsApp Automation

Automated messaging to @usernames, phone numbers, and groups across Telegram and WhatsApp hands-free.

Autonomous Planning

Breaks complex multi-step commands into logical sub-tasks, executes sequentially, and dynamically replans on failure.

Full Device Control

Brightness, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, flashlight, alarms, timers, calendar, calls, SMS — all controlled autonomously.

4-Tier Knowledge Graph

Temporary, long-term, learned patterns, and hardware-encrypted sensitive data for personalized intelligence.

Screen Extraction & Vision

Read and remember meeting details or notes from any screen using multimodal vision models and accessibility trees.
